# BCH Argentina NFT benefits

Planned benefits for the collection.

## Support BCH adoption in Argentina

Buying an NFT directly from BCH Argentina supports Bitcoin Cash adoption in Argentina and the community's work to promote its use as money.

## Credit toward an annual Club membership

Buying a new NFT directly from BCH Argentina allows you to apply 100% of the purchase price as credit toward an annual BCH Argentina Club membership.

- The credit is recorded in US dollars using the BCH exchange rate applied at the time of purchase, together with the transaction.
- It can be used to join or renew within 12 months of purchase.
- Multiple direct purchases can accumulate credit up to the cost of one annual membership. Any excess is neither refunded nor carried forward to later years.
- The credit belongs to the original buyer, can be used once, and does not transfer when the NFT is resold.
- Secondary-market purchases do not generate this credit.
- The buyer keeps the NFT after using the benefit.

Membership prices referenced in the proposal are US$50 and US$100 per year on [bcharg.com](https://www.bcharg.com/). For example, a US$30-equivalent NFT purchase leaves US$20 to pay for a US$50 membership, or US$70 for a US$100 membership.

**Suggested wording:** “Buy your capybara directly from BCH Argentina and apply your purchase payment toward your next annual Club membership.”

**Accounting note:** the amount applied is an advance membership payment. It must not be counted both as NFT revenue and as a separate full membership payment.

## ARG Tokens airdrops

BCH Argentina NFT holders receive ARG Tokens airdrops.

Amounts, dates, eligibility conditions, and distribution methods will be defined and announced for each airdrop. This benefit applies to holders, including secondary-market buyers, subject to the conditions announced for each distribution.
